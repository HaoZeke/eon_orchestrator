#+title: Vinyl Alcohol → Acetaldehyde Tautomerization
#+date: 2026-03-13

* Overview

Vinyl alcohol (enol) tautomerizes to acetaldehyde (keto) via a 1,3-hydrogen
shift. This is a classic example of keto-enol tautomerization and demonstrates
IRA alignment value for hydrogen transfer reactions.

* Source Structures

Reactant and product structures are from:

#+begin_quote
R. Goswami, M. Gunde, and H. Jónsson, "Enhanced climbing image nudged elastic
band method with hessian eigenmode alignment," Jan. 22, 2026, arXiv:
arXiv:2601.12630. doi: [[https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2601.12630][10.48550/arXiv.2601.12630]].
#+end_quote

* Why This Demonstrates IRA Value

The 1,3-hydrogen shift involves:
- H atom migration from O to C (or vice versa)
- C=C double bond shifting to C=O
- Significant electronic reorganization
- 8 atoms with non-trivial correspondence

Without IRA alignment:
- H atom may be incorrectly mapped
- Initial path may have unrealistic H positions
- NEB may converge to wrong mechanism

With IRA alignment:
- Correct H-atom correspondence identified
- Smooth interpolation of H-transfer coordinate
- Realistic initial path for NEB optimization

*Note*: This is a high-barrier reaction; enol form is metastable.

** Reaction Mechanism

Concerted 1,3-H shift:
- H migrates from O to C (or C to O)
- C=C π bond becomes C=O π bond
- Planar transition state
