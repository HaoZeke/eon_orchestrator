1 Related Software
------------------

NEB Orchestrator builds on the following software:

- `eOn <https://eondocs.org>`_ - Nudged Elastic Band engine

- `Snakemake <https://snakemake.github.io>`_ - Workflow management system

- `rgpycrumbs <https://rgpycrumbs.rgoswami.me>`_ - Alignment and visualization tools

- `PET-MAD <https://huggingface.co/lab-cosmo/pet-mad>`_ - Machine learning potential
